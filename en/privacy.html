PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

1. Overview

Iro no Sanpo (the “App”) is designed to work on your device without an account. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

2. Information not sent to us

Photos, colors, notes, and creation dates you record in the App are stored on your device. The App has no feature for sending, sharing, or publishing those records to our server.

The App does not collect location data, contacts, analytics data, or advertising identifiers. It does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

3. Camera and Photo Library

The App asks for Camera or Photo Library permission only when you choose to take or select a photo. Access is used only to help you save a color in your own Color Book. You can save colors and notes without granting permission or adding a photo.

4. App Store purchase

The App is provided as a one-time paid app on the App Store. It does not use in-app purchases, advertising, or subscriptions. Apple processes payment, and we do not receive your payment method or Apple Account credentials.

5. Contact

For questions about this policy or the App, use our support page or email takashi8959+ironosanpo@gmail.com. Please do not include photos, location information, your name, address, or other personal information that is not needed for your request.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated.
